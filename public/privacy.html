Privacy policy

What we collect on praneethlabs.com, how advertising and cookies work on our sites, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Who we are

Praneeth Labs (“we”, “us”) runs this website, praneethlabs.com, and the products linked from it. You can reach us at praneeth.thommandru@gmail.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers praneethlabs.com itself. Each of our products also has its own privacy policy, which explains what that product does with your data in detail. Where a product has its own policy, that policy applies when you use the product:

  • PL Tools — tools.praneethlabs.com/privacy
  • PaperFinder — paperfinder.praneethlabs.com/privacy
  • StockWatcher — stockwatcher.praneethlabs.com/privacy

The section on advertising and cookies below applies to every Praneeth Labs website that shows ads.

Information we collect on praneethlabs.com

  • No accounts or forms. This site has no sign-up, login or forms, so we don't ask you for personal information.
  • No cookies of our own. We don't set cookies on praneethlabs.com and we don't run our own analytics here.
  • Hosting and security logs. The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to help with your request, and we don't sell it or add it to mailing lists.

Advertising and cookies

Some Praneeth Labs websites show ads to keep our free tools free. We use Google AdSense to serve these ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our websites or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using a consent message provided through Google. You can change your choice at any time from that message.

Ads are always labelled “Advertisement”. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the sites still work without them.

How we use information

We use the limited information described above to run and secure our websites, answer your messages, and understand and fix problems. We don't sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need them to help you, and delete them when they're no longer needed. Hosting logs are kept by Cloudflare under its own retention periods.

Children

Our websites are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us at praneeth.thommandru@gmail.com and we'll respond as soon as we can.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to other websites, including our own products and third-party sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be explained here.
